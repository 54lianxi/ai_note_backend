Privacy Policy and Personal Data Text

“WhimNote Application”

Update Date: October 11, 2026

1. Objective

WhimNote (“WhimNote”, “we”, “us”, or “Company”) is an AI-powered multimodal note-taking application that helps you capture, transcribe, summarize and search your information using text, voice recordings, images and conversations. We process your personal data in accordance with general principles of privacy and the provisions of the applicable data protection legislation, including, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) 2016/679 and other applicable legislation.

Your personal data that you have provided / will provide to our Company, and/or that is obtained by our Company by any external means, may be processed by our Company as “Data Controller”:

  • in connection with, and limited and measured by, the objective of processing your personal data;
  • by maintaining the accuracy and up-to-date status of the personal data as reported or notified to our Company;
  • by recording, storing, preserving and reorganizing it, and transferring it to institutions authorized to request such personal data by law, and classifying and sharing it with third parties within or outside the country under the conditions stipulated by legislation and, where required, upon your explicit consent.

This Privacy Policy describes which data we collect, how we intend to use, store, protect and share the data we collect, how you can withdraw your consent for the processing of these data, and how you can correct and revise the data. This Privacy Policy forms part of our Terms and Conditions. Capitalized terms in this Policy shall have the meanings specified in the Terms and Conditions unless defined separately in this Policy.

Company does not share this information with third parties except as necessary to provide our service. At any time you can request the deletion of this information; your other rights are outlined in this Privacy Policy.

We collect information by fair and lawful means, with your knowledge and consent. We also let you know why we are collecting it and how it will be used. You are free to refuse our request for this information, with the understanding that we may be unable to provide you with some of your desired services without it. While using the Application, you may provide information through third-party applications or services (such as Apple Sign-In, Google Sign-In and third-party AI services); please be aware that such third parties apply their own terms and privacy policies, and Company is not responsible for those policies.

  • execution of information security processes and activities to ensure business continuity;
  • compliance with legislation and protection of persons’ rights, privacy and safety, and prevention of crimes and other illegal acts;

  • informing you about new products, services and applications, and, with your consent, conducting marketing activities.

b) Legal Reasons

  • it is necessary to process your personal data, provided that we establish a contractual relationship with you, or that it is directly related to our performance obligation arising from this contract;
  • we have to process data in order to establish a right for you, to exercise and protect this right;
  • processing is necessary for our legitimate interests, provided that your fundamental rights and freedoms are not harmed;
  • conditions that are necessary in order to fulfill our legal obligation;
  • your consent, or your explicit consent where required by law.

We do not permit the use of our Application by children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect or process personal data from anyone under the age of 16. If you learn that someone under the age of 16 has provided us with personal information, please contact us. 5. Transferring Personal Data to Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. Where necessary to provide our core features and to achieve the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, we may transfer your personal data to service providers of our Company and to third parties embedded in our service, whose processing is subject to their own privacy policies, including:

  • AI service providers, used to transcribe and summarize audio recordings, understand images, generate responses, and perform vectorized retrieval;
  • sign-in services such as Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In, used for account registration and identity verification;
  • infrastructure service providers, used for data storage and backup (such as object storage, database and cloud servers);
  • authorized public institutions and organizations that are entitled by law to request such data, for compliance with legislation, monitoring and execution of legal affairs.

Because we may use servers and cloud systems located abroad, your personal data may be transferred outside your country or region. We will take the appropriate lawful measures required by applicable legislation and safeguard the security of your personal data before any such cross-border transfer.

6. Data Security Measures

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including but not limited to:

  • Encryption: sensitive data is stored with cryptographic methods and, if required, transferred through environments encrypted with cryptographic methods (such as HTTPS/TLS).
  • Access control: access to sensitive data follows the principle of least privilege and is protected by authentication.
  • Logging: transaction records regarding sensitive data are securely logged and audited.
  • Backup: Company periodically backs up the data it stores, using the facilities provided by the cloud infrastructure providers.
  • Personnel management: non-disclosure agreements are concluded with employees taking part in sensitive personal data processing, and security training is provided at regular intervals.

In the event that your personal data is damaged as a result of attacks on the Application or on the Company system, despite Company taking the necessary information security measures, or the personal data is obtained by unauthorized third parties, Company notifies this situation to Users immediately and, if necessary, to the relevant data protection authority, and takes necessary measures.
