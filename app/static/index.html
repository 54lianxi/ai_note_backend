What is WhimNote

One place for every idea you capture

WhimNote is an AI-powered multimodal note-taking application. It lets you capture information as text, voice recordings, photos, screenshots and documents, then automatically transcribes audio, extracts key information from images, writes concise summaries, and organizes everything into a searchable personal knowledge base. A built-in AI assistant answers your questions using only the notes and recordings you have chosen to save.

How WhimNote uses your Google data

When you choose “Sign in with Google”, WhimNote requests only the basic profile information needed to create and secure your account — your name, email address and profile picture. We use it solely to identify you and let you sign in.

  • We do not access Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar or any other Google Workspace data.
  • We do not sell your Google user data or share it for advertising.
  • Google user data is never used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or ML models.
  • You can revoke access at any time and request deletion of your data.

Transparency first. Our Privacy Policy explains exactly what we collect, how it is used, how long it is kept, and the rights you have over your data.
