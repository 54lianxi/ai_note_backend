Privacy Policy

“WhimNote Application”

Effective Date: October 12, 2026  ·  Last Updated: October 12, 2026

WhimNote (“WhimNote”, “we”, “us” or “Company”) provides an AI-powered multimodal note-taking application. This Privacy Policy explains, clearly and specifically, what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, how we protect it, and the choices and rights you have. It also describes our handling of information received from Google APIs. This Privacy Policy forms part of our Terms of Service.

Summary of what we collect and how we use it:

  • What we collect: account details (name, email, profile picture) from Apple/Google sign-in; the notes, voice recordings, transcripts, images and files you create; and basic device and diagnostic data.
  • Why we collect it: to create your account, provide transcription, summarization, image understanding, chat and search, to keep the service secure, and to support you.
  • How it is used: only to run and improve the app for you. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train generalized AI/ML models.
  • Who we share it with: sign-in providers, AI processing providers, and cloud hosting/storage providers that work on our behalf.
  • How to delete it: delete individual notes or your whole account in the app, or email support@whimnote.app.

1. Who we are

WhimNote is the data controller responsible for the personal data described in this Privacy Policy. You can contact us at any time at support@whimnote.app.

2. Data we collect

We collect only the data we need to provide the app. We do not collect data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data. The categories of data we collect are:

CategoryWhat it includesWhy we collect it
Account & identity data Your display name, email address, profile picture and account identifier, received when you sign in with Apple or Google. To create and secure your account, sign you in, and sync your notes across devices.
Your content Notes and text you write; questions you ask the AI assistant; images, files, links and documents you upload; and the AI responses and summaries generated for you. To store your notes and provide transcription, summarization, image understanding, conversational and search features.
Audio & transcripts Voice recordings you make, the transcripts generated from them, and related data. To transcribe your recordings and produce summaries.
Device & usage data IP address, device name, operating system and app version, approximate region, timestamps, and diagnostic/error logs. To operate the service securely, keep it reliable, prevent abuse and fix problems.
Support data Messages and contact details you send us when you ask for help. To respond to your questions and requests.

We do not collect government ID numbers, financial account or payment card data (payments, if any, are handled by the app stores or external payment processors), health data, precise location, or contacts from your device. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through the app.

3. How we use data

We use your personal data only to:

  • create and manage your account and let you sign in;
  • provide the core features: recording, transcription, summarization, image understanding, AI chat and search over your own content;
  • store, sync and display your notes on your devices;
  • keep the service safe, secure and reliable, including abuse prevention and troubleshooting;
  • provide customer support and respond to your requests;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service; and
  • communicate with you about the service (for example, important changes).

We do not use your content or Google user data to train, develop or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. The legal bases for processing are, as applicable: performance of our contract with you, your consent, our legitimate interests in operating a safe service, and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Google user data & Limited Use

When you sign in with Google, we receive your basic Google profile — name, email address and profile picture — through Google Sign-In. We use this information solely to authenticate you and create your account. We do not request, access or store any other Google data (such as Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar or Google Photos).

WhimNote’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We limit our use of Google user data to providing or improving features that are visible and prominent in the app (sign-in and account access).
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide or improve those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • We do not use Google user data to train generalized AI/ML models, and no humans read it except for security, abuse, or with your consent.

5. How we share data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run WhimNote, and only as needed:

  • Sign-in providers — Apple and Google, to authenticate you;
  • AI processing providers — used to transcribe audio, summarize text and understand images, which means your submitted content is processed by these providers on our behalf;
  • Cloud infrastructure providers — for hosting, storage and backups;
  • Analytics/diagnostics providers — to keep the app stable and secure; and
  • Legal and safety — with authorities or third parties when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the public.

All service providers are bound to process your data only for the purposes we specify and in line with this Privacy Policy.

6. International transfers

WhimNote uses servers and cloud services that may be located outside your country or region. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we take the measures required by applicable law (such as appropriate safeguards and contractual protections) to keep your data protected.

7. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above:

  • Your content (notes, recordings, transcripts, images) is kept until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Account data is kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days after you delete your account.
  • Device & diagnostic logs are kept for a limited period (generally up to 12 months) for security and troubleshooting.
  • Support and legal records may be kept longer where required by law.
  • Backups are overwritten or deleted on a rolling schedule.

8. Security

We protect your data with appropriate technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), access controls based on least privilege, authentication, secure storage, logging and auditing, regular backups, and confidentiality obligations for staff. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to notify you and the relevant authorities if a breach affecting your data occurs.

9. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • delete your data or close your account;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time; and
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@whimnote.app. We will respond within the time required by applicable law, and generally within 30 days. You can also revoke WhimNote’s access to your Google or Apple account at any time in your account’s security settings.

10. How to delete your data

  1. In the app, open your profile/account and delete individual notes, or choose “Delete account”.
  2. Or email support@whimnote.app from the address associated with your account and request deletion.
  3. To revoke sign-in access, go to your Google Account “Third-party apps with account access” or your Apple ID settings and remove WhimNote.

After deletion, your content is removed from active systems and deleted from backups on a rolling basis.

11. Children

WhimNote is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it. Users under 18 must have permission from a parent or legal guardian.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new “Last Updated” date, and where changes are material we will notify you in the app or by email. Continued use of WhimNote after an update means you accept the revised policy.
