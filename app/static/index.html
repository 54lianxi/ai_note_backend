Purpose of the application

What WhimNote does and why

WhimNote is a personal note-taking application that uses AI to help people capture and organize information. Its purpose is to let you save anything you want to remember as text, a voice recording, a photo or a document, then automatically make it useful and easy to find again. Specifically, WhimNote is used to:

🎙️

Record & transcribe

Capture voice notes, meetings and ideas and turn them into accurate text transcripts.

📝

Summarize

Condense long recordings and text into short summaries you can review in seconds.

📷

Capture images & documents

Snap a whiteboard, receipt or page and extract the important details from it.

💬

Ask your notes

Chat with your own saved content and get answers grounded in what you stored.

🔍

Semantic search

Search by meaning, not just keywords, to resurface the right note from months ago.

☁️

Organize & sync

Keep your notes organized and available across your devices, and delete them anytime.

WhimNote is a general-purpose productivity tool for note-taking and personal knowledge management. It is intended for users to store and revisit their own notes and recordings.

Google user data

How WhimNote uses your Google data

If you choose “Sign in with Google”, WhimNote requests only the basic profile information needed to create and secure your account — your name, email address and profile picture. We use it solely to identify you and let you sign in.

  • We do not access Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Photos or any other Google Workspace data.
  • We do not sell your Google user data or share it for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not use Google user data to train, develop or improve generalized AI or ML models.
  • Only the account data listed in our Privacy Policy is collected, and you can request its deletion at any time.

Transparency first. Our Privacy Policy explains exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over your data.

Compliance

Our commitment to safe, compliant use of Google APIs

WhimNote accesses Google user data only through Google Sign-In, for the limited purpose of authenticating you and creating your account.

  • WhimNote’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • WhimNote uses Google APIs only for the purposes described above, and never for advertising, resale, or building user profiles.
  • WhimNote does not use Google APIs, and does not allow its users to use the app, to create, generate, request, promote or facilitate AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (AI NCII), deepfakes, or any other content that violates the Google APIs Terms of Service or applicable law.
  • Content that is unlawful, deceptive, or that violates the rights of others is not permitted and will be removed; accounts that violate these rules may be terminated.
Your data, your control

Access or delete your data at any time

You own the notes you create. You can review, export or delete your content inside the app, and you can ask us to delete your account and the personal data associated with it.

  1. Open WhimNote and go to your account/profile, then delete individual notes, or use “Delete account”.
  2. Or email us at support@whimnote.app with your request.
  3. You may also revoke WhimNote’s access to your Google account at any time in your Google Account permissions.

Data we collect at a glance:

  • Account info (name, email) via Apple/Google sign-in
  • Notes, recordings, transcripts, images and files you create
  • Basic device and log data needed to run the service
