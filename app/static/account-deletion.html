2. How to request account deletion

  1. Fastest: do it yourself in the app — open Me, tap Delete account and confirm. Your account plus all notes, chats and uploaded files are deleted right away, no email needed.
  2. Send the request from the email address linked to the WhimNote account you want deleted — this is how we verify that the account belongs to you.
  3. Send it to 54kuliqi@gmail.com with a subject such as “Account deletion request”.
  4. Please include the account email and your reason for the request (see section 3).
  5. We complete processing within 7 business days and reply to confirm the outcome.

3. Information you need to provide

ItemDetails
Account email The email address linked to the WhimNote account you want deleted, so we can confirm the account belongs to you.
Reason for the request Why you want to delete the account — no longer using it, switching accounts, privacy concerns, etc. A short sentence is enough.

If you want to delete only part of your data (for example a few notes) instead of the whole account, just say so in your email and we will take care of it.

4. Processing time

We process deletion requests within 7 business days of receiving them and reply by email to confirm the outcome. If the request is incomplete (for example, it was not sent from the account’s email address, or the account email or reason is missing), we will reply asking you to complete it, and the 7 business days restart once we receive the missing information.

5. What happens after deletion

  • Your account and everything in it — notes, voice recordings, transcripts, images, attachments and chat history — is deleted.
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone, so please back up or export anything you want to keep first.
  • You can no longer sign in to WhimNote with that account; to keep using WhimNote, create a new account.
  • Google / Apple sign-in access is revoked as well. You can also remove WhimNote’s access yourself at any time in your Google Account or Apple ID settings.

2. 如何申请注销账号

  1. 最快的方式：直接在 App 内注销 —— 打开「我的」页，点击注销账户并确认， 账号以及全部笔记、对话、已上传的文件会立即删除，无需发邮件。
  2. 请使用你想要删除的那个账号所绑定的邮箱（也就是你登录 WhimNote 用的邮箱）来发送申请邮件。 用该邮箱发送是为了核验你的身份，避免他人误删你的账号。
  3. 收件人：54kuliqi@gmail.com，邮件主题建议写「账号注销申请」。
  4. 邮件内容需要提供：账号邮箱和申请理由（详见第 3 节）。
  5. 我们收到申请后会在 7 个工作日内处理完成，并通过邮件告知处理结果。

3. 需要提供的信息

信息说明
账号邮箱 要注销的 WhimNote 账号所绑定的邮箱，用于确认账号归属。
申请理由 你希望注销账号的原因，例如不再使用、更换账号、隐私考虑等，简单说明即可。

如果你只想删除部分数据（比如某几条笔记）而不是注销整个账号，也可以在邮件里说明，我们会按你的要求处理。

4. 处理时限

我们会在收到你的注销申请后 7 个工作日内处理完成，并通过邮件回复处理结果。 如果申请信息不完整（例如没有使用账号绑定邮箱发送、缺少账号邮箱或申请理由），我们会回信请你补充， 处理时间从你补充完整后重新计算。

5. 注销后会发生什么

  • 账号本身以及该账号下的全部内容——笔记、录音、转写文字、图片、附件、对话记录——都会被删除。
  • 删除后无法恢复；如果需要保留内容，请先自行备份或导出。
  • 注销后无法再用该账号登录 WhimNote，如需继续使用请重新注册一个新账号。
  • 注销完成后，Google / Apple 第三方登录授权会一并失效（你也可以随时在 Google 账号或 Apple ID 设置里手动移除 WhimNote 的授权）。
